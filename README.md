### What I keep coming back to

Pages where the scrollbar is the playback control, not just a way to move down —
a film scrubbed by scroll, a house built as you read, a courtyard changing season.
Half the list above is the same idea explored from a different angle.

The other half is small tools that do one job and need no server to do it.
